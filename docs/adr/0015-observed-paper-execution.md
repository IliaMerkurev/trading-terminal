# ADR 0015: Observed-price paper execution through the retained engine

Status: implemented; independent account, durable funding queue and actual observed-price integration checks pass.

Reuse NautilusTrader 1.231.0, the existing Profile sizing/risk gateway, position policy and protection module. Paper observations stream as executable quotes into the same engine; do not build another account engine, multiply spot PnL by leverage, or feed the historical O/L/H/C path as real ticks. The adapter consumes shared IR conditions rather than computing another strategy. Signal transition notifications remain separate from the retained position policy.

Each observed trade price gets explicit adverse fractional slippage and tick rounding. The virtual quote assumes sufficient liquidity, immediate processing and no exchange queue; paper fills do not claim exchange equivalence. Mark price values perpetual equity/maintenance, while the trade quote remains the execution source. Observed funding is a separate event. At an observation, funding and liquidation precede stop/take, then eligible strategy orders; no same-observation protection exit/reentry. Gaps execute protections at the first available observation, never an invented threshold price.

Persist each observation and normalized account/fill result in a separate additive journal. Replay reconstructs account state with fresh capital and the same ordered observations. Closing monitoring does not liquidate a virtual position or invent an end-of-backtest fill. Any missing live interval pauses paper; reconstructed M1 candles only recover signal state, not executable ticks. Explicit continuity revalidation accepts continuation at the next observation, with missing execution disclosed. Failed paper recording must not continue an unjournaled account; the injected commit-failure test verifies this boundary.

Bound each engine session to 10,000 retained orders and 250,000 journaled paper observations, then require review instead of unbounded native cache growth. UI history/logs are bounded and paginated; journal growth and sustained-session performance require measured evidence. These are resource limits, not a claim that the application is an always-on trading service.

Independent expected outcomes include: spot buy 100/sell 110 with 0.1% fees yields 1009.79 from 1000; a two-unit perpetual short 100→90 with +2 funding and .38 fees yields 1021.62. A 95 stop after observing 100 then 80 fills at 80. Separate mark 80 can liquidate at executable trade 100. The retained historical regression suite checks that extracting the shared position policy does not alter old results.

Confirmed funding publication can lag the settlement boundary. Journal real quotes before account processing and query the public funding-history endpoint asynchronously. If the exact boundary rate is not yet published, retain observations in order and display a waiting state; strategy signals continue. Apply the confirmed rate at the first recorded post-boundary observation using its observed mark. Never substitute the ticker estimate or zero. Resume the account queue without charging funding twice after restart. Twelve bounded lookup attempts lead to an explicit error requiring review. An unobserved funding boundary beyond the tolerated 60-second interval cannot be silently resumed.

A signal can submit a paper order only on a quote received after its availability and whose provider timestamp is at least the signal boundary. The quote visible before a late confirmed candle is not an executable future observation. One historical O/L/H/C path is never passed off as observed market ticks.
